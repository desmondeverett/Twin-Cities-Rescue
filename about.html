Our Story

Founded in 2015, Twin Cities Animal Rescue began as a small network of dedicated volunteers passionate about saving homeless and vulnerable pets in Minnesota. Over the past decade, we have grown into a comprehensive foster-based rescue organization serving the entire Minneapolis-St. Paul metropolitan area.

We work tirelessly to provide medical care, rehabilitation, and loving temporary homes for animals while finding them their forever families.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat every animal with kindness, dignity, and individualized care.
  • Integrity: We operate with complete transparency, honesty, and financial accountability to our community.
  • Community Collaboration: We believe in the power of strong partnerships with local shelters, veterinary clinics, and animal advocates.
  • Lifelong Commitment: We ensure every pet we rescue receives support for as long as it takes to find a safe home.
