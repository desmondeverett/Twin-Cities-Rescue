Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming abandoned, neglected, and surrendered animals across the Minneapolis-St. Paul metro area. We believe every animal deserves safety, respect, and a forever family.

Our Impact

Thanks to our dedicated community, we continue to make a real difference for local pets in need:

  • 1,200+ animals rescued and provided care last year alone.
  • 95% adoption success rate for rescued pets.
  • 350+ active foster homes supporting our mission.

How You Can Help

Whether you are looking to add a new member to your family, offer a temporary foster home, or volunteer your time, there are many ways to support our mission.

  • Adopt: Give a rescue pet the loving home they deserve.
  • Foster: Provide temporary care and shelter for pets in transition.
  • Volunteer: Help us with daily care, events, and administrative support.
