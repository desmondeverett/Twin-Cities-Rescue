Our Community Programs

At Twin Cities Animal Rescue, we offer several ways for individuals and families to get involved and support local animals in need. Learn more about our core services below.

Adoption Process

Our adoption program matches animals with loving, permanent homes. We work to ensure a safe and happy fit for both the pet and the adopter.

Steps to Adopt:

  1. Browse Available Pets: Explore our current adoptable dogs and cats online or at local partner events.
  2. Submit an Inquiry: Fill out our interest form to share your lifestyle, living situation, and pet preferences.
  3. Matchmaking & Meet-and-Greet: Meet with an adoption coordinator and the pet to ensure a good fit.
  4. Finalize Adoption: Complete the adoption agreement, pay the standard adoption fee, and bring your new family member home!

Foster Program

Foster homes provide vital temporary shelter, medical care, and socialization for rescued animals while they await permanent adoption.

Why Foster?

  • Save Lives: Fostering frees up shelter space and gives pets a comfortable home environment.
  • All Supplies Provided: We cover food, crates, medical care, and necessary supplies.
  • Flexible Commitments: Fostering can range from a few days to a few months depending on your schedule.

Volunteer Opportunities

Volunteers are the heart of our organization. We have roles available for people with a wide variety of skills and availability.

  • Animal Care Specialist: Assist with daily feeding, exercise, and enrichment for animals in transition.
  • Event Coordinator: Help plan, set up, and manage weekend adoption events across the metro area.
  • Transport Volunteer: Safely transport animals to vet appointments or foster homes.
  • Administrative Support: Assist with processing adoption applications and managing community outreach.

Interested in joining our team or becoming a foster parent? Get in touch via our interest form.
